## My interest in Software Engineering

My interest in software engineering really started with robotics and CyberPatriot in high school, and it's grown since then through coursework at the University of Hawaii. I'm especially drawn to cybersecurity, specifically roles like a security analyst, because the problem-solving side is interesting, like figuring out how systems are made and how to defend against them.

## Learning skills

Looking ahead, I want to keep building both my technical depth and my practical skill set. My coursework in Java, TypeScript, and discrete math has given me a strong foundation; however, I still need to work harder on problem-solving and strengthening those skills. I also want to start developing my web development skills using things like React (components, state, hooks) and much more. Lastly, I want to start getting into more of the specific security tools being used.

## Experiences

Alongside building skills, I want to gain more hands-on experience that puts them into practice. This means working through online platforms and sites focused on cybersecurity practice, building small security-focused projects like scripts that can check for common vulnerabilities or misconfigurations, and eventually pursuing internships or entry-level roles in the field. I think practical experience is more helpful to me than just coursework because being able to actually implement it while learning about it helps the concepts stick better.
