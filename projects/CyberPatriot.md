## The importance

While this isn't a formal "project," I would consider it one of my most significant endeavors and something that made me interested in computer science. In high school, I was part of the JROTC program, and one of the teams within it was CyberPatriot. Basically, it was sort of a competition within JROTC programs where you had to log into a competition virtual machine and score points by fixing different vulnerabilities within the system. 

The vulnerabilities ranged from simple admin permissions to deeper issues that needed terminal access and much harder coding. As a high schooler who hadn't been exposed to much of this before, it was both easy and difficult. The harder vulnerabilities gave me trouble while the easy ones let me get lots of points. Looking back, this was really my first hands-on introduction to cybersecurity (even though it was very basic, beginner-level), and it made me interested in pursuing it further.
