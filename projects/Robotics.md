## The Experience

My first real exposure to hands-on STEM work was through robotics. I did a middle school robotics class, and later, in high school, I was part of a VEX Robotics team through JROTC for about a year. Over that time, I partially filled a few of the roles, like programming (using block-based coding), building some parts of the robot, and eventually being team lead for one competition. This gave me a mix of technical and leadership experience, as well as working in a group.

As team lead, I helped my teammates with any issues they were having and supported them throughout our competition. Even though we were beginners, our robot actually performed pretty well in the competition, and I think that our teamwork was what really helped. Robotics was another part of why I decided to pursue computer science because the coding aspect was really fun, and working with others was great.
